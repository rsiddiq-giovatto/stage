FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the T-Mobile Partner Program through KORE

What is the T-Mobile Partner Program?

The T-Mobile Partner Program allows MSPs, VARs, telecom agents, system integrators, and solution providers to sell T-Mobile for Business solutions, including wireless connectivity, Business Internet, and IoT services. Partners work through authorized primary partners like KORE to access tools, support, and commission opportunities.

How do I become a T-Mobile partner?

You typically work through an authorized primary partner such as KORE. The process includes submitting a partner inquiry, completing onboarding requirements, gaining access to sales and marketing resources, and registering opportunities through the partner program.

What is a T-Mobile sub-agent?

A T-Mobile sub-agent sells T-Mobile for Business solutions through a primary partner such as KORE. Sub-agents receive access to products, commissions, training, and sales support while leveraging the infrastructure and expertise of an established partner organization.

What is a T-Mobile Pinnacle Primary Partner?

Pinnacle Primary Partner is one of T-Mobile’s highest partner designations. Working with a Pinnacle Primary Partner like KORE provides access to experienced support, enablement resources, and deeper knowledge of T-Mobile for Business solutions.

Can I sell T-Mobile Business Internet through KORE?

Yes. Partners can offer T-Mobile Business Internet for businesses seeking primary connectivity, backup internet, temporary connectivity, or rapid deployment options. KORE helps partners identify opportunities, position solutions, and support deployments.

Can I sell IoT solutions in addition to wireless connectivity?

Yes. KORE helps partners expand beyond traditional wireless offerings by combining T-Mobile connectivity with IoT solutions such as fleet management, asset tracking, connected healthcare, industrial IoT, smart cities, and remote monitoring.

What types of businesses can join the T-Mobile Partner Program?

The program is designed for MSPs, VARs, telecom agents, distributors, consultants, system integrators, and other technology solution providers that want to add wireless, internet, or IoT services to their portfolio.

What support does KORE provide to T-Mobile partners?

KORE provides business development support, onboarding assistance, deal registration guidance, training, marketing resources, commission support, and access to the KORE Channels Partner Portal. Partners also gain access to IoT expertise that can help expand customer opportunities.

What are the benefits of working with KORE as a T-Mobile partner?

Partners gain access to T-Mobile for Business solutions alongside KORE’s experience in connectivity and IoT. Benefits include enablement, sales support, marketing resources, deal registration assistance, and the ability to offer wireless and IoT solutions through a single relationship.

What are the requirements to join the T-Mobile Partner Program through KORE?

Most partners need a valid business entity, tax ID, company website, business email domain, and business address. Additional onboarding requirements may vary based on your organization and the solutions you plan to offer.

Do I need IoT experience to become a T-Mobile partner?

No. Many partners begin with wireless or Business Internet and later expand into IoT. KORE provides guidance, training, and solution expertise to help partners identify and grow IoT opportunities over time.

How do commissions work for T-Mobile partners?

Partners may qualify for commissions on eligible T-Mobile for Business solutions, including wireless and Business Internet services. Compensation structures vary by partner model and current programs. A KORE Business Development Manager can provide details specific to your business.

Can I become a T-Mobile reseller?

Yes. Many organizations participate in the T-Mobile ecosystem as agents, sub-agents, resellers, or solution providers through authorized primary partners like KORE.

What is the difference between a T-Mobile agent and a T-Mobile sub-agent?

A primary agent maintains a direct relationship with T-Mobile and supports partner enablement. A sub-agent works through a primary partner and gains access to T-Mobile solutions, resources, and commissions through that relationship.

Can MSPs sell T-Mobile services?

Yes. Managed service providers can add T-Mobile wireless, Business Internet, and IoT solutions to their portfolio to create recurring revenue opportunities and support customer connectivity needs.
